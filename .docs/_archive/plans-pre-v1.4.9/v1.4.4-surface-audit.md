## Methodology

Each candidate surface was classified by walking five signals in order:

1. **Router presence.** Is the component path imported and bound to a route in `src/router.tsx`? Routed → eligible for active or deferred-reports. Not routed but referenced by a route → consolidation candidate / legacy. Not routed at all → inactive.
2. **Nav reachability.** Is the route reachable from the app shell — `handleNavNavigate` route map (today/week/emails/dropbox/actions/me/people/accounts/projects/settings) plus first-class drill-down from those nav targets (entity detail, meeting detail, action detail, reports launched from entity pages)? Hidden-only-via-URL routes are flagged in Open Questions.
3. **`.docs/design/INVENTORY.md` state.** `referenced` / `spec` / `covered-by` / `legacy` / `documented` / `gap`. Drives carry-forward vs active classification when the surface has a spec but not yet a route.
4. **`_pending-inventory-updates.log`.** Three entries: one fake page (`FakePage.tsx`), two worktree-path noise re-touches of `PersonDetailEditorial.tsx`. No unreconciled real surfaces — log is effectively empty as of this audit.
5. **`wp-foundation-roadmap-reorientation.md` §v1.4.4 wave hints.** W2 entity / W3 briefing / W4 action / W5 system+history / v1.4.8 reports. Wave assignment in the Active list maps each shipped surface to the wave that owns it.

**Substrate-consumption probe.** For each active surface, the primary `invoke<...>("<tauri_cmd>")` calls were skimmed to anchor the substrate column (ability/service). This is the "what does the WP block need to call" column; it is not exhaustive.

**Drift check against INVENTORY.md.** INVENTORY.md and `src/router.tsx` agree on 26 routes and all classifications surveyed. No drift detected between the two. The "consolidation candidates" called out in INVENTORY.md (AccountDetailEditorial legacy, MeetingHistoryDetailPage wrapper, ReportPage vs dedicated reports) are reproduced in this audit's Inactive/legacy and Deferred-to-v1.4.8 lists.

**Counts:** Active = 16. Carry-forward = 0. Inactive/legacy = 2 (1 file-level + 1 route-level wrapper). Deferred-to-v1.4.8 = 4. Open questions = 4.

## 5. Open questions — RESOLVED 2026-05-20

James's calls, locked at audit close:

1. **Report-shaped surfaces beyond the 4 named — DEFER ALL 8 TO v1.4.8.** BookOfBusiness, EbrQbr, SWOT, AccountHealth, RiskBriefing, WeeklyImpact, MonthlyWrapped, generic ReportPage shell. They share the report-generation lifecycle; splitting them across v1.4.4/v1.4.8 fragments the report substrate work.

4. **DailyBriefingDSpine — ONE SURFACE.** Design exploration on the same `/` route, not a separate surface. The W3 DailyBriefing block chooses between current and D-spine treatments per design review.
