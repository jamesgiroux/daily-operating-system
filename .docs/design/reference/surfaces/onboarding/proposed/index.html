Proposed: three things, then you're in.

Strip onboarding to the minimum that lets DailyOS actually work — connect Google, install Claude Code, pick a role. Everything else (your card, first account, briefing prime, dashboard tour) happens lazily once you're inside the app.

8 → 3 steps 2 connectors + 1 preset turmeric tint preserved

Shape A

Single screen

One panel, three tiles, one CTA. No step counter.

Open full size →
  • Whole setup visible at once — feels short by construction.
  • Tiles double as ongoing status; same surface works in Settings later.
  • One CTA to commit; no "are we done yet" ambiguity.
  • Google OAuth bounce away/back makes the page reload mid-task — single panel has to gracefully restore.
  • Three actions on one screen can feel busier than three calm steps.

Shape B

Sequential, 3 steps

One purpose per screen, ~10 seconds each. Step bar at the top.

1 · Google
2 · Claude
3 · Role
Step 1 → Step 2 → Step 3 →
  • Each screen has one job; OAuth bounce returns to a clean step 1 → step 2.
  • Calm pacing — empty space carries the brand voice.
  • Closer to today's flow, lower risk of regression on the auth dance.
  • Three screens still feels like three screens, even at 30 seconds total.
  • Step bar adds chrome that single-screen avoids.
