# v1.5.0 W4 - L0 Plan Packet: Briefing and Meeting Composition Surfaces

> **Status:** Draft for L0 review. Wave W4 of the v1.5.0 Composable Surfaces program (`.docs/plans/v1.5.0-waves.html`).
> **Prerequisites:** W1-W3 merged to `dev`; W1 established the Tauri React composition renderer and Account producer; W2 established layout overlays without changing composition authorship; W3 generalized detail composition routing through Account, Project, Person, and Action Detail.

---

## §0 - Origination and scope

- **Origination class:** Extension. W4 carries the composition substrate into the forward-looking surfaces: Daily Briefing and Meeting Detail.
- **Scope tier:** Wave. The wave plan names briefing and meeting-prep/recap producers as the largest forward-surface slice and requires both producer output and rendered proof on real app data.
- **Trust topology:** `local-to-local single-user`, with Amendment 3 paths. W4 touches composition-producing abilities, first-party Tauri IPC, service-owned meeting and briefing reads, claim/provenance/trust output, freshness rendering, feedback, and user-facing surfaces.
- **Review posture:** AC-scoped. Blockers are acceptance-criteria misses, ADR-named contract violations, or PR-introduced regressions. Path-alpha issues route to Codebase Maintenance unless they directly invalidate this packet.
- **Proof obligation:** Daily Briefing and Meeting Detail render end-to-end from substrate-authored `Composition` output through `get_projected_composition`, with provenance, trust bands, freshness, real data, and existing mutation/edit/feedback affordances preserved.

---

## §1 - Why W4 exists

W1 proved Account Detail. W3 proved the detail routing model is not Account-specific. W4 proves the composition substrate works for the forward surfaces that make DailyOS feel like a chief of staff: the daily briefing and meeting prep/recap flow.

These surfaces are not static entity dossiers. They combine schedule state, readiness, prep, transcript/outcome signals, continuity, commitments, role movement, predictions, and post-meeting findings. W4 therefore must prove that the composition model can express time-sensitive forward work without regressing trust, provenance, freshness, or the user actions already present on the shipped pages.

W4 does not introduce new analytical reasoning just because the surface has new block names. The wave plan explicitly allows new blocks such as `plan-adherence`, `predictions-vs-reality`, `role-changes`, and `coaching`, but only pattern-first and only when an existing source ability/service already produces the analysis. Any net-new analysis moves to v1.5.1.

## §2 - K-in citations and constraints

- **ADR-0130 Surface-Independent Composition Contract:** abilities author `Composition`; surfaces render projected blocks; unknown blocks degrade safely; provenance references point into the canonical ability envelope.
- **ADR-0105 Provenance as First-Class Output:** source attribution, field attribution, and `source_asof` are product semantics, not decoration.
- **ADR-0108 Provenance Rendering and Privacy:** provenance rendering must avoid raw source leaks and respect sensitivity constraints across DOM, command JSON, logs, diagnostics, and screenshots.
- **ADR-0093 Prompt Injection Hardening:** meeting transcripts, calendar content, notes, and external document text are untrusted inputs. W4 must not add prompt channels or bypass existing sensitivity/prompt-channel gates.
- **ADR-0065 Meeting Prep Editability:** Meeting Detail user agenda and user notes remain editable user-owned fields. Composition is the read/provenance model, not a reason to remove edits.
- **ADR-0043 Meeting Intelligence Is Core:** meeting intelligence is not an optional add-on. W4 must preserve the core meeting workflow and not hide shipped recap intelligence behind a "producer later" branch.
- **ADR-0081 Event-Driven Meeting Intelligence:** meeting intelligence is always-on and permanent; Daily Briefing assembles/narrates pre-existing meeting intelligence rather than generating a throwaway one-off prep surface.
- **ADR-0082 Entity Generic Prep Pipeline:** meeting prep should consume existing prep and entity context substrate rather than a one-off meeting surface pipeline.
- **ADR-0112 Migration Strategy - Parallel Run and Cutover:** `prepare_meeting` and `get_daily_readiness` are ability migration targets. W4 should wrap existing producers/readers and preserve compatibility rather than forcing an all-at-once cutover.
- **ADR-0113 Human and Agent Analysis as First-Class Claim Sources:** human and agent-authored analytical outputs must be represented with source class, lifecycle, and trust treatment.
- **ADR-0115 Signal Granularity Audit:** invalidation must be explicit enough that corrections, stale prep, transcript changes, and meeting outcome changes refresh derived surfaces.
- **docs/solutions/security-issues/prompt-channel-sensitivity-class-sweep-2026-05-18.md:** prompt-channel sensitivity is centralized in `services/claims.rs`; W4 must not fork sensitivity rules.

No cited prior solution replaces W4. The relevant prior substrate is ADR-0130 plus the W1-W3 producers/router and existing briefing/meeting intelligence abilities.

## §3 - Binding decisions

### Decision A - W4 adds two producer families

W4 adds two composition producer families:

| Surface | Producer | Subject kind | Composition id |
|---|---|---|---|
| Daily Briefing | `dailyos/daily-briefing` | Briefing | `dailyos/daily-briefing:briefing:{workspace_scope}~{yyyy-mm-dd}` |
| Meeting Detail | `dailyos/meeting-detail` | Meeting | `dailyos/meeting-detail:meeting:{meeting_token}` |

The router extends `ProducerSubject` with:

- `Briefing { workspace_scope, date }`
- `Meeting { meeting_token }`

The parser rejects empty ids, malformed or extra delimiters, control characters, unknown producer prefixes, malformed dates, malformed workspace scopes, malformed meeting tokens, and producer/subject mismatches such as `dailyos/daily-briefing:meeting:{id}`.

`workspace_scope` is a non-sensitive stable token, not the configured workspace path. W4 accepts exactly `local` as the briefing workspace token:

- accepted id: `dailyos/daily-briefing:briefing:local~2026-06-02`
- rejected ids: any subject id containing `/`, `\`, `:`, `@`, whitespace, control characters, empty date/scope, malformed date, or a workspace path

The parsed briefing subject serializes only `workspace_scope`, `date`, `composition_id`, `schema_version`, and `expected_composition_version`. A Tauri-only producer-input hydration step then reads the current app config and injects `workspace_id` for the existing `get_daily_briefing` backing ability immediately before `TauriAbilityBridge::invoke_tauri_app`. This keeps local filesystem paths and raw config values out of composition ids, projection cache keys, logs, diagnostics, and frontend hooks while preserving the current ability contract.

The frontend never derives a composition id from `get_config().workspacePath`. It uses the stable id shape `dailyos/daily-briefing:briefing:local~{yyyy-mm-dd}`. The legacy DTO briefing hook may keep reading config as compatibility fallback only.

`meeting_token` is a non-sensitive composition token, not the configured calendar provider id, raw `DbMeeting.id`, meeting title, transcript filename, or workspace path. W4 accepts only server-issued tokens matching `mtg_[0-9a-f]{16,32}`. The parser rejects any meeting subject id containing `/`, `\`, `:`, `@`, `.`, whitespace, control characters, empty token, provider-looking ids, path-like values, or existing `_at_` calendar-id sanitization artifacts.

The parsed meeting subject serializes only `meeting_token`, `composition_id`, `schema_version`, and `expected_composition_version`. A Tauri-only producer-input hydration step resolves `meeting_token` to the local persisted meeting row immediately before producer invocation. Raw meeting ids and calendar provider ids remain out of composition ids, projection cache keys, diagnostics, logs, and frontend projected-composition state. The frontend must not interpolate the route `meetingId` or `CalendarEvent.id` into a composition id; it requests or receives a server-issued meeting composition token, while legacy DTO and mutation calls may keep using the existing raw meeting id compatibility path.

W4 adds a service-owned, read-only meeting composition token issuer/resolver. The issuer may accept the existing route `meetingId` through a Tauri-only compatibility call or include the token in an existing meeting-detail response, but it returns only `meeting_token` to the projected-composition hook. The resolver fails closed when the token is unknown, stale, or no longer maps to a local meeting row.

### Decision B - Daily Briefing wraps the existing briefing ability

The existing read-only `get_daily_briefing` ability remains the backing source for Daily Briefing compatibility. W4 adds a composition-producing wrapper that calls the same service-owned daily briefing producer and emits `CompositionKind::Briefing`.

Daily Briefing composition sections:

- `headline`
- `schedule`
- `attention`
- `readiness`
- `follow-through`
- `trust-and-sources`
- empty/degraded states

The wrapper preserves the existing no-auto-enqueue, no provider-backed synthesis, and no mutation posture. It renders source count, `source_asof`, trust summary, freshness, meeting cards, attention items, refresh affordances, and current empty states from projected composition when available.

### Decision C - Meeting Detail uses one custom composition kind across prep and recap states

W4 adds one `dailyos/meeting-detail` producer that emits the correct composition shape for the current meeting state:

- future or in-progress meeting with prep state
- past meeting without transcript/outcomes
- past meeting with transcript/outcomes and recap intelligence

The producer uses `CompositionKind::Custom { type_id: "dailyos/meeting-detail" }` for all Meeting Detail output. `PrepPack` is too narrow because W4 proof explicitly spans prep and recap. Meeting state is represented in section ids, block payloads, and render hints, not by changing the composition kind per state.

The producer consumes existing meeting intelligence sources:

- `src-tauri/src/services/meetings.rs::get_meeting_intelligence`
- existing post-meeting intelligence, continuity, prediction scorecard, interaction dynamics, champion health, commitments/actions, and role-change service readers
- existing prep source loading and agenda/user-note state

W4 must move any command-level ad hoc read needed by the producer behind service-owned snapshot readers. Commands and frontend components do not author composition. Projection must not call command helpers that perform writes, emit feedback, or update derived state as a side effect of a read. In particular, prediction scorecard data for composition must be computed through a side-effect-free service reader; the existing command path that emits prediction feedback is not a valid producer input path.

### Decision C.1 - Meeting recap data enters composition through a typed sourced snapshot

Existing post-meeting DTOs are not sufficient block inputs by themselves because they lack field-level attribution, `source_asof`, claim refs, sensitivity, and trust metadata. W4 therefore adds a service-owned `MeetingCompositionSnapshot` contract before composing Meeting Detail blocks.

Every recap/prep field consumed by the composition producer is wrapped as a sourced field:

- `value`
- `field_path` within the composition producer output
- `source_label`
- `source_ref` when available
- `source_asof`
- `sensitivity`
- `trust_band`
- `claim_refs`
- `provenance_kind`
- `feedback_target` when existing feedback can act on the field

The snapshot reader may reuse existing DTO structs internally, but it must transform them into sourced fields before block construction. Block builders create `ProvenanceRef` values only for `field_path`s that resolve into the `AbilityOutput<Composition>` field attribution envelope. If a recap field cannot be wrapped with source/freshness/trust metadata, that block degrades visibly or is omitted by the producer with a source-gap block; the frontend does not silently render it as trusted intelligence.

Minimum source rules:

- transcript-derived interaction dynamics, findings, role changes, champion health, talk balance, quotes, and enriched captures use transcript/capture source refs plus transcript processed time or capture timestamp for `source_asof`
- prediction scorecard uses side-effect-free prep/outcome service readers, prep source refs plus outcome capture refs, and is trust-capped unless both sides have freshness
- user agenda and notes are user-authored sourced fields and preserve edit routes
- meeting time, room, title, and transcript availability are display facts with snapshot provenance and freshness rather than claim refs
- any coaching or plan-adherence field without an existing source ability output is deferred to v1.5.1

### Decision D - New blocks are wrappers unless existing analysis already exists

W4 starts with existing shipped patterns and block primitives:

- `PostMeetingIntelligence`
- `FindingsTriad`
- `SignalGrid`
- `TalkBalanceBar`
- `CommitmentRow`
- W1/W3 primitives such as `FreshnessIndicator`, `TrustBandBadge`, `ClaimSummary`, `EvidenceList`, `ActionList`, and custom block fallback

Net-new W4 block candidates:

| Candidate | W4 rule |
|---|---|
| `plan-adherence` | Allowed only if existing prep/outcome comparison data already supports it. Otherwise defer to v1.5.1. |
| `predictions-vs-reality` | Allowed because existing prediction scorecard data is already loaded by Meeting Detail. |
| `role-changes` | Allowed only from existing role-change output/readers. |
| `coaching` | Allowed only if existing output already has coaching guidance with provenance/trust/freshness. No new LLM coaching in W4. |

Every analytical block must pass the 5-question Intelligence Loop check in §5.

### Decision E - Renderer integration is composition-first with compatibility fallback

W4 extends `useProjectedComposition` subject support to briefing and meeting subjects. The pages render composition-backed sections first when projected data is available, while keeping DTO-backed rendering as a compatibility fallback for loading, error, or temporary producer failure during W4.

The frontend preserves:

- Daily Briefing shell, `FolioBar`, refresh, meeting cards, action affordances, finite ending, and existing event-driven refreshes
- Meeting Detail transcript sync/attach/paste/reprocess, agenda draft, prep field edits, prefill, refresh briefing, user agenda/notes, feedback, actions, dialogs, toasts, navigation, and finite editorial ending with `FinisMarker`

Mutation controls continue to call existing commands/services. Successful mutations force-refresh or invalidate the projected composition rather than mutating frontend-authored block state.

## §5 - Intelligence Loop check

1. **Claim model:** Analytical meeting/briefing facts use existing `IntelligenceClaim` records or existing source ability outputs that carry claim refs/field bindings. Snapshot-only facts such as meeting time, meeting room, prep status, and transcript availability are display facts with provenance/freshness wrappers. W4 does not add frontend-only analytical data.
2. **Provenance + trust:** Composition outputs carry canonical provenance on `AbilityOutput<Composition>`. Blocks use `ProvenanceRef`, `claim_refs`, trust bands, source labels, and `source_asof`. Missing provenance degrades visibly rather than disappearing.
3. **Signals + invalidation:** Meeting prep, transcript, post-intelligence, action, agenda, notes, claim lifecycle, entity updates, calendar updates, workflow completion, and daily readiness changes invalidate or force-refresh derived compositions. Missing signal emission discovered in W4 is implemented in W4.
4. **Runtime + surfaces:** Tauri consumes `ProjectedComposition` through `get_projected_composition`. MCP and signed surfaces fail closed unless actor scope and sensitivity allow the same producer context. Producers use service readers; commands do not author compositions or perform producer-only direct DB assembly.
5. **Feedback loop:** Existing feedback and correction controls remain claim-backed where claims exist. User edits route through service mutations. Dismissals, corrections, corroborations, contradictions, and action edits refresh trust/freshness and projected composition state.
